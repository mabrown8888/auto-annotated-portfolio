## The Facebook Marketplace Special

Every golfer has to start somewhere. For me, that somewhere was a Facebook Marketplace listing for an odd-only iron set. You know—3, 5, 7, 9. At the time, it seemed like a steal. I mean, how hard could it be to hit with just the odd irons, right?

Wrong.

## The Great 7-Iron Incident

A few months into my golf journey, disaster struck. My trusty 7-iron—the backbone of any decent iron set—snapped. Just gone. Now I'm stuck with a golf bag that looks like it's missing teeth: 3, 5, ~~7~~, 9.

And let me tell you, the gap between a 9-iron and a 5-iron is no joke. Anything from 120-160 yards? That's a 9. Anything above 160? Better pull out the 5 and pray. There's this massive dead zone in my game where I'm either coming up short or launching it into the next county.

## The Leslie Park Saga

Today was a perfect example. I rolled up to Leslie Park Golf Course with my buddy, ready to play some best ball and maybe—just maybe—put together a decent round.

**Front 9:** Shot a 47. Not amazing, but honestly pretty solid for me. I was feeling good, getting into a rhythm, thinking "this might be the day."

**Back 9:** Complete disaster. Shot a 55. Well, I think it was a 55. I lost count somewhere around hole 14 after chunking my third shot into a bunker with my 5-iron because I didn't have a 7.

## The Cost of Defeat

The real kicker? My partner and I lost the best ball match. You know what that means? I had to buy my friends Maize & Blue sandwiches. At $15 a pop. That's $30 worth of sandwiches because my 7-iron decided to retire early.

Those sandwiches would've tasted a lot better if we'd won.

## The Goal

Despite all this—the incomplete iron set, the inconsistent rounds, the expensive sandwich debt—I've got a goal: **break 97 for the first time**.

I know what you're thinking. "Just buy a new iron set." And you're probably right. But there's something about this challenge that keeps me coming back. Maybe I'm stubborn. Maybe I'm cheap. Maybe I just really want to prove I can do it with what I've got.

So here's to the next round. Here's to hopefully remembering to keep count on the back 9. And here's to one day breaking 97 with the most dysfunctional iron set in Michigan.

Wish me luck. I'm gonna need it.
