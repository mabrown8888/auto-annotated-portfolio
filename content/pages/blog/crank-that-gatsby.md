## The Theme Crisis

Let me tell you something about throwing a themed party: coming up with the theme is the hardest part. Hands down.

I must have spent weeks brainstorming ideas. A normal person would've just said "house party" and called it a day. But not me. I needed something unique, something memorable, something that would make people say "wait, what?"

Then it hit me: **Crank That Gatsby**. A mashup of The Great Gatsby's roaring 20s elegance and Soulja Boy's iconic "Crank That" era. Was it ridiculous? Absolutely. Did it make perfect sense in my head? Also yes.

Most importantly of all—this theme did NOT come easy. I went through probably 15 different ideas before landing on this one. But once I had it, I was all in.

## The Planning Begins

Once the theme was locked in, my inner project manager took over. And by "took over," I mean I turned into a full-blown event coordinator who probably should've been charging for my services.

**The Partiful:** I set up the guest list on Partiful, carefully curating who would vibe with a Great Gatsby x Soulja Boy fusion. Every detail mattered—the description, the timing, the vibe. I spent way too long getting the event page just right.

**The Balloons:** I bought balloons. Gold ones. Black ones. Lots of them. Because what's a Gatsby party without excessive decor?

**The Layout:** I reorganized my entire apartment. Furniture moved here, furniture moved there. I mapped out the flow—where people would enter, where they'd hang out, where the music would be, optimal standing room. I had a literal floor plan in my notes app.

**The Timeline:** I didn't just invite people—I organized their arrival times. Early crew at 8, main wave at 9, fashionably late group at 10. I basically created a guest queue management system.

## The Execution

Party day came, and I was in full micromanagement mode. I'm talking about:
- Checking the playlist 47 times
- Adjusting lighting at least 10 times
- Making sure the balloons were at the perfect height
- Confirming arrival times with people
- Stress-eating while setting everything up

But you know what? All that micromanaging paid off.

![Party setup](/images/crankdat3.jpeg)

## The Payoff

The party was awesome. Like, genuinely awesome. People showed up in flapper dresses and headbands mixed with throwback Soulja Boy era fits. The music was perfect—switching between jazz standards and 2007 hip-hop bangers. Everyone had a great time, and I got about a dozen messages the next day saying it was one of the best parties they'd been to.

Was it extra? Absolutely. Did I spend way too much time planning a single party? Without question. Would I do it all over again? In a heartbeat.

![Party in full swing](/images/crankdat4.jpeg)

## The Takeaway

I learned something about myself through this whole experience: I can't help but turn everything into a full-scale project. Whether it's coding an app, building a trading bot, or throwing a party—if I'm doing it, I'm doing it at 110%.

Also, if you ever need a theme for a party, just mash up two completely unrelated things and see what happens. Worst case? You get a funny story. Best case? You get Crank That Gatsby.

10/10 would micromanage again.
